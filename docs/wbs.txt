System Apply late payment fees The system should calculate and apply late payment fees.
- If a monthly payment is not received within 15 calendar days after the 
due date, a late fee will be charged on the 16th day. The late fee will be 
$5.00 or 5% of the unpaid installment amount, whichever is less. Only one 
late fee will be charged for each missed payment.
- If an account is more than 90 days past due, it will be considered in 
default. When this happens, the full past due balance must be paid within 
30 days to avoid loan acceleration.
- If an account is more than 120 days past due, the loan will be 
accelerated. When this happens, the full remaining loan balance including 
interest and outstanding fees must be paid within 30 days of receiving a 
notice of default. The APR remains at 1%.
